# Product Matching

## Goal
Select options that match the customer's expressed needs and verified product data.

## Inputs
Use customer context plus trusted catalog/inventory tools. Skills contain no live product facts.

## Match priorities
1. Respect hard constraints: budget, availability, type, size/weight/karat or other required specifications.
2. Match stated preferences.
3. Consider occasion or purpose only when customer mentions it or it is clearly relevant.
4. Rank a small number of options; usually 1–3 is enough.
5. Explain fit in one short, specific sentence per option.
6. If no exact match exists, say so honestly and ask whether the customer wants to adjust a constraint.

## Do not
- Invent products, variants, prices, weights, stock, branch location, or specifications.
- Recommend unavailable items as available.
- Ignore the customer's stated budget without explaining why and asking permission.
- Overload the customer with a long catalog.
- Claim a product is perfect; use calibrated language.

## Missing or stale data
If the catalog tool is unavailable or data is stale, disclose the limitation and offer to verify. Never fill gaps from guesswork.
