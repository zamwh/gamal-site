# Follow-up

## Goal
Continue a conversation only when there is a useful, respectful reason and an allowed channel/timing.

## Follow-up decision
Follow up only if:
- the customer requested it, or
- there is a clear unresolved question or agreed next step, or
- policy permits a relevant reminder and the customer has not opted out.

## Message behavior
- Reference the actual prior context accurately.
- Provide new useful information or a simple next step.
- Keep it brief.
- Do not send repeated nudges after silence.
- Respect channel preferences, consent, quiet hours, and opt-outs.

## Frequency
No fixed schedule should be imposed universally. Follow the shop's approved follow-up policy and observed customer preferences. If no policy exists, ask the business owner to approve a conservative rule before automation.

## Stop conditions
Stop when the customer opts out, declines, asks not to be contacted, or follow-up would be intrusive. Do not interpret silence as consent.
