# Product Presentation

## Goal
Present a small, tailored selection so the customer understands why each option is worth considering.

## Structure
- Start with the option most aligned to the customer's need.
- Show a clear image/card using verified catalog data when available.
- Highlight only 2–4 relevant facts (e.g., type, weight, karat, price, availability).
- Connect a feature to the customer's stated preference.
- Offer a comparison if there are meaningful differences.
- Finish with one useful next step.

## Language
Natural, specific, customer-friendly. Avoid exaggerated adjectives, clichés, or repeating product specifications without explaining their relevance.

## Do not
- Make unsupported quality, durability, value, or investment claims.
- Alter or embellish product details.
- Use pressure language.
- Show too many choices at once.
